What it is like here

Six things that are true, including the demanding ones.

Written so you can rule us out quickly if it does not fit. That is a legitimate outcome and it saves everyone a fortnight.

Seniority means responsibility, not distance

Our principals write code and sit in client sessions. Nobody here graduates out of the work into a purely commercial role.

You will be trusted early

You will own a system, talk directly to the client who depends on it, and have the authority to say no. Support is available; permission is assumed.

We protect focus

Two meeting-light days a week, asynchronous by default, and a genuine expectation that you are offline when you are off.

Learning is scheduled, not aspirational

Ten percent of your time is yours for research, certification or open-source work, with a budget attached rather than a slogan.

We hire for judgement

Tool knowledge is teachable. The ability to tell a client something they do not want to hear, kindly and with evidence, is what we interview for.

Distributed, deliberately

Remote-first across North America and Europe, with quarterly gatherings that are worth flying for. Where you live is not a career variable.

The package

Specific numbers, not adjectives.

Every benefit below is written the way it appears in our handbook. If something is a range, we say so rather than calling it competitive.

  • Salary benchmarked to the top quartile, reviewed annually and published internally in bands
  • Genuinely flexible hours, remote-first across North America and Europe
  • Ten percent research time with an annual learning budget
  • Full health, dental and vision cover for you and your dependants
  • Home office allowance and hardware you actually choose
  • Minimum four weeks leave, and we track that you take it
  • Parental leave of sixteen weeks at full pay for every parent
  • Quarterly in-person gatherings with travel covered

How hiring works

Four conversations. None of them free work.

We do not set unpaid take-home projects and we do not ask algorithm puzzles. Everything you do in our process is a real problem from our domain, worked through with someone who does the job.

  1. 0145 min

    Conversation

    Forty-five minutes with someone who does the work. Two-way — bring hard questions.

  2. 0290 min

    Craft session

    A real problem from our domain, worked through together. Not an algorithm quiz, and never unpaid project work.

  3. 0360 min

    Judgement session

    A scenario with no clean answer. We want to see how you reason and how you disagree.

  4. 04≤ 5 days

    Offer

    Decision within five working days of the last session, with the band and the reasoning explained.
