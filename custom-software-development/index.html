The problem

You are paying twice: once for the licence, once for the workaround.

The platform covers 70% of what you do. The other 30% lives in spreadsheets, a shared mailbox and one person who knows the exception. That gap is where your margin, your data quality and your ability to change quickly all quietly disappear.

What it costs you

  • Process steps that exist only to work around the software
  • Critical logic living in spreadsheets nobody has reviewed
  • Per-seat costs rising faster than the value delivered
  • Roadmap decisions made by a vendor with different priorities
  • Integration work repeated every time the vendor changes an API

What we build

The parts that make it survive production.

Every engagement includes all of this. None of it is an upgrade tier.

Product discovery

We map the operation, the constraints and the economics before proposing a build — including the case for buying instead.

System architecture

Boundaries, data ownership and failure modes designed deliberately, with the trade-offs written down as decision records.

Backend engineering

Typed, tested services with clear contracts, sensible transactions and observability from the first commit.

Frontend engineering

Interfaces that are fast on real hardware, usable with a keyboard and readable with a screen reader. Accessibility is not a phase.

Data engineering

Schema design, migration strategy and reporting pipelines that keep working as volume grows by an order of magnitude.

Integration

Resilient connections to the systems you keep — with retries, idempotency and a documented contract for each one.

AI where it earns its place

Extraction, classification, search and generation added where they measurably beat the deterministic alternative, and left out where they do not.

Quality engineering

Automated tests at the levels that matter, load testing against realistic traffic, and a pipeline that blocks regressions rather than reporting them.

How it runs

From first conversation to running system.

  1. 01Phase 1

    Discovery

    Workshops with the people who do the work, a systems and data map, and a costed recommendation — which sometimes says do not build this.

    2–3 weeks

  2. 02Phase 2

    Architecture and proof

    The design, the decision records, and a thin slice through the riskiest part of the system to prove the approach before scale-up.

    2 weeks

  3. 03Phase 3

    Build in the open

    Two-week increments, always deployable, demonstrated to real users. You see progress in a running system rather than a status report.

    8–20 weeks

  4. 04Phase 4

    Launch and hand over

    Staged rollout with a rollback path, then documentation, runbooks and training so your team can own it outright.

    2 weeks + support

How we price it

Three ways in. A stop point at each one.

For well-defined scope we quote a fixed price and hold it. For genuinely exploratory work we run time-boxed increments with a hard budget and a stop point at every boundary.

Discovery

Fixed price · 2–3 weeks

The systems map, the architecture, the estimate and an honest build-versus-buy recommendation. Yours to take anywhere.

  • Stakeholder and user workshops
  • Systems and data mapping
  • Solution architecture
  • Costed delivery plan and build-vs-buy analysis
